Universal interview prep tips

1. Solve, then re-solve

Re-attempt a problem 2–3 days later without looking. That's where retention happens.

2. Talk while you code

Practise narrating your approach out loud — it's exactly what interviewers expect.

3. Master patterns, not problems

Sliding window, two pointers, BFS/DFS, DP — group problems by pattern.

4. Time-box practice

30 minutes max per problem. Then read the editorial. Move on. Revisit later.

5. Mock interview weekly

Simulate the real thing — stranger, camera, whiteboard or shared editor.

6. Track everything here

If it isn't logged, it didn't happen. Use this tracker every single day.
